HOW TO PLAY

The visitors already posted their runs. You bat the bottom halves and have three innings to beat them.

Tap the field, press Swing, or hit Space when the ball reaches the plate. Early pulls the ball, late pushes it the other way. Press Bunt (or B) before the pitch arrives to square around. Press P or Esc (or the pause button) to pause.

CONContact. Widens your timing window, so near-misses still find the barrel.
POWPower. More exit velocity and more lift on contact.
SPDSpeed. Beats out grounders, stretches singles, scores from second.
DEFDefense. The visitors' range, reactions and error rate.
VEL · MOV · CTLTheir pitcher's velocity, break on the slider and changeup, and how often he finds the zone.
