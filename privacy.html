Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Pocket Diamond is a free browser baseball game made by Imugi Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. In short: we collect what we need to run accounts, ranked play and the game itself, plus gameplay stats to make the game better. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

If you make an account: your email address and password (used only to sign you in; the password is stored by our sign-in provider and we can't see it), your username, and your saved progress: your team, cards, lineup, coins, playoff run and settings.

If you play ranked: your matches and their results, the plays in them (pitches, swings, plate appearances), your rating and its history, and your season stats. Your username, rating, record and stats appear on the public leaderboard and your public profile.

Gameplay stats (optional): games you start and finish and how they go, screens you visit, settings you use, and errors, plus a sample of individual plays, used to balance the game and fix bugs. These are tied to a random ID, or to your account ID and username if you're signed in. They never include your email. Our analytics provider is set not to keep your IP address and keeps only your country. You can turn this off any time in Options → Share stats.

Feedback you send: your message, the contact you choose to leave (if any), and basic details to help us reproduce a bug: browser, screen size, game version and what you were doing in the game.

Connection data: like any website, our hosting, sign-in and font providers receive your IP address and browser details when your browser connects to them, and may keep them briefly in their logs for security and to keep the service running.

On your device: the game saves your progress and settings in your browser's local storage so it works offline and as a guest. Clearing your browser data removes it. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To run the game and your account, match ranked players and keep ratings and leaderboards fair (including spotting cheating), save your progress across devices, answer feedback, fix bugs and improve the game. We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the providers below.

Who helps us run the game

Vercel (hosting), Supabase (accounts, cloud saves, ranked play and feedback), PostHog (gameplay stats) and Google Fonts (the game's fonts). Each handles data only to provide its service to us. Their servers may be in the United States or other countries.

How long we keep it

Account data and saves: until you delete your account. Ranked results: for as long as the leaderboards and season history they belong to are kept. Gameplay stats: up to two years. Feedback: as long as it's useful for fixing and improving the game.

Your choices and rights

You can play as a guest without an account, turn gameplay stats off in Options, and change your username (once every 30 days). You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and the personal data tied to it. When an account is deleted, its ranked games may stay in other players' match history without your username. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and you can always contact us about them.

Children

Pocket Diamond is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Players under 13 should play as a guest and not create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Connections to the game are encrypted, passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, and access to the data is limited. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, let players know in the game.

Contact

Use the Feedback button in the game, or reach Imugi Labs at imugilabs.com. For account deletion or data requests, include your username.
